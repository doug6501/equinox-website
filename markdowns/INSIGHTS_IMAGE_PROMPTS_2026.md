# Insights hero image prompts — 2026 refresh

Equinox Audio Visuals editorial photography series for `assets/insights-*.jpg`.
Generated for article heroes and insights hub cards. Mid-article images use real venue photos (see IMAGE_VARIATION_SUMMARY_APR_2026.md).

## Art direction bible

Shared style line (append to every prompt):

> Premium event-production photography for a boutique AV company in Vermont. 35mm editorial, quiet luxury. Warm bone highlights, charcoal ink shadows, subtle ember and amber practical lights (console LEDs, tungsten uplight — not neon). Shallow depth of field. Natural New England venue atmosphere.

**Hard negatives (every prompt):**
- No readable text, logos, brand names, UI chrome, watermarks, or sparkles
- No purple/indigo AI glow, neon mesh, cyberpunk blue, or holographic overlays
- No fake product brands on gear
- No warped hands or extra fingers when people appear

**Aspect:** 16:9 landscape
