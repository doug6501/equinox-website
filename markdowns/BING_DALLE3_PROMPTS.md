## 📋 **BING DALL-E 3 OPTIMIZATION TIPS**

### **Key Phrases for Realism:**
- "Professional photography"
- "Realistic photography style"
- "Natural lighting"
- "Authentic atmosphere"
- "No futuristic elements"
- "No cartoon styling"
- "Real event"
- "Actual business environment"

### **Avoid These Terms:**
- "Futuristic"
- "Sci-fi"
- "Cartoon"
- "Anime"
- "Digital art"
- "Concept art"
- "3D rendering"
- "Fantasy"

### **Recommended Settings in Bing:**
- **Style**: Choose "Photographic" or "Realistic"
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 or 7:4 if available
- **Quality**: High resolution
- **Avoid**: Artistic, Stylized, or Abstract styles

### **Implementation Steps:**
1. Go to `bing.com/images/create`
2. Copy the exact prompt for each page
3. Generate the image
4. Download and save with the specified filename
5. Add to your assets folder

**These prompts are specifically designed for DALL-E 3 to produce realistic, professional photography that matches your website's authentic, professional brand!** 📸✨
