"Vanish" Film Screening

Creating a premiere cinematic experience for a documentary screening at a historic museum.

The Vision

The Bennington Museum hosted a special screening of the documentary "Vanish," which explores the disappearing historic barns of Vermont. The event required a professional, cinematic presentation that would honor the film's subject matter and provide the audience with a true theater-quality experience within the museum's educational center.

The Solution

Our team installed a large-format, professional-grade cinema screen and a high-lumen laser projector to ensure a bright, crisp image. We designed a surround-sound audio system to create an immersive auditory experience for the film's score and narration. The setup was engineered to be clean and unobtrusive, respecting the historic space while delivering a modern cinematic presentation.
