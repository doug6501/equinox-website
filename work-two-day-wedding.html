Hill Farm and Hildene — Manchester, VT — August 22–23, 2025

Two days. Two venues. One continuous weekend with no visible boundary between them.

Two days. Two venues. One weekend for a couple and their families that needed to feel continuous, not divided. The rehearsal dinner at Hill Farm called for warmth and ease — string lights around the grounds, a wireless microphone and indoor sound for toasts, the kind of setup that disappears into the evening. We finished that in the morning. By the time guests arrived, the cables were hidden and the lights were on. Hill Farm catered.

Day two at Hildene was a different scope. Lincoln Hall carries history in its proportions, and the band brought their own sound; our focus was the room’s visual atmosphere. Perimeter uplighting, string lights synced to the music, dance floor lighting, and stage wash for the band. Warm amber tones that worked with the woodwork rather than against it.

Real-time lighting programming meant an engineer at the console through the band’s set — adjusting as the music shifted, not running a fixed preset. The lighting was felt more than noticed. Pangaea Restaurant in North Bennington catered the reception.

Diwan by Design ran the logistics across both days. We handled the technical layer as one continuous production so day two didn’t feel like starting over.
