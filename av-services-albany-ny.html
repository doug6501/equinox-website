AV & Event Production Services in Albany, NY

Albany's Trusted Audio Visual Partner

Albany sits at the crossroads of New York's Capital Region — a city built on government, education, and a thriving cultural scene that demands polished, professional event production. From black-tie galas at the Empire State Plaza to intimate wedding receptions in the city's historic neighborhoods, Equinox Audio Visuals brings the technical expertise and personal attention that Albany events deserve. We've worked with planners, venues, and organizations across the Capital Region, and we understand what it takes to deliver flawless production in this market.

The Albany Capital Center and Times Union Center host some of the region's highest-profile corporate conferences, fundraisers, and conventions. These events require more than basic AV — they need engineered sound design, intelligent lighting that adapts to the program flow, precise video switching, and reliable live streaming for remote audiences. That's exactly what we do. Our team arrives early, tests everything, and stays through the last handshake so you never have to think about the technology.

Whether you're planning a product launch downtown, a wedding at one of Albany's restored historic venues, or a multi-day conference that needs to run like clockwork, we bring the same level of care to every event. Based in Manchester, Vermont, we're just a short drive across the border — close enough to know your venues, far enough to bring a fresh perspective that Capital Region clients appreciate.

Our Services in Albany

Sound & Audio
Clean, reliable speech reinforcement for conferences at the Albany Capital Center, full-range concert sound for galas at the Times Union Center, and wireless microphone systems tailored to your venue's acoustics.
Lighting Design
Architectural uplighting for the Empire State Plaza's grand spaces, elegant wash lighting for wedding receptions, intelligent moving fixtures for corporate stage productions, and custom color palettes that match your brand or theme.
Video & Projection
High-lumen projection for large ballrooms and convention halls, LED video walls for trade shows and conferences, IMAG camera systems for keynote presentations, and content playback for award ceremonies.
