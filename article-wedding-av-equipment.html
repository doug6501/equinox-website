Wedding AV Equipment: What Actually Matters

PREV_INSIGHT
6 min read
Weddings

By Doug Kunnath

Date April 2025

You've spent months planning every detail—the flowers, the dress, the menu. But here's what most couples overlook until it's too late: the AV. And trust us, nothing ruins a ceremony faster than guests straining to hear your vows, or a reception where the speeches are drowned out by feedback.

Great AV is invisible. When it's done right, nobody notices the equipment—they just experience a flawless event. When it's done wrong? Everyone notices. Let's make sure your wedding falls into the first category.

How to Assess Your Wedding AV Needs and Goals

Before you start shopping for gear or hiring vendors, take a step back. What kind of wedding are you actually throwing? A 50-person garden ceremony needs different AV than a 300-person ballroom reception. Here's what matters:

Key Questions to Ask Yourself:

  • What is the theme and style of your wedding? Is it formal or casual, traditional or modern, indoor or outdoor?
  • What is the size and layout of your wedding venue? How many guests are you expecting, and how will they be seated and arranged?
  • What is the schedule and agenda of your wedding day? How long is it, and what are the main segments and activities, such as ceremony, reception, speeches, dances, etc.?
  • What is the mood and atmosphere you want to create? Do you want it to be romantic, fun, elegant, etc.?
  • What is your budget and timeline for your wedding day? How much can you afford to spend on AV equipment, and how much time do you have to plan and prepare?

Essential Wedding AV Equipment

1. Microphones and Speakers

Audio is non-negotiable. If your guests can't hear the vows, what's the point? Here's what you actually need:

  • Wireless microphones for the officiant, bride, groom, and speakers
  • Ceremony sound system to ensure all guests can hear vows and readings
  • Reception sound system for music, speeches, and announcements
  • Backup equipment in case of technical issues

2. Lighting Equipment

Lighting transforms a space. It's the difference between "nice venue" and "wow, this is magical." Here's what works:

  • Uplighting to enhance venue architecture
  • Dance floor lighting for the reception
  • String lights for outdoor or rustic venues
  • Spotlights for the ceremony and cake cutting

3. Visual Equipment

Want to share your love story or include remote guests? Visual tech makes it happen:

  • Projectors and screens for photo slideshows or live streaming
  • Flat panel monitors for larger venues or outdoor ceremonies
  • Live streaming setup for remote guests

How to Choose the Best AV Partner for Your Wedding

Equipment is only half the equation. The other half? The people running it. Here's how to find an AV partner who won't let you down on the biggest day of your life:

Experience and Reputation

Weddings aren't the place for "learning on the job." Find someone who's done this hundreds of times:

  • Portfolio of successful wedding events
  • Client testimonials and reviews
  • Professional certifications and awards
  • References from previous wedding clients

Equipment and Services

Make sure they bring the full package—not just gear, but support:

  • Complete range of wedding AV equipment
  • Professional setup and breakdown services
  • On-site technical support during your event
  • Backup equipment and contingency plans

Communication and Collaboration

You're juggling a thousand details. Your AV partner should make your life easier, not harder:

  • Communicate clearly and regularly throughout planning
  • Understand your vision and expectations
  • Provide detailed quotes and contracts
  • Conduct site visits and rehearsals

Wedding AV Planning Timeline

6-12 Months Before

  • Research and contact potential AV partners
  • Schedule venue visits with AV team
  • Discuss your vision and requirements
  • Review quotes and contracts

3-6 Months Before

  • Finalize AV equipment selection
  • Plan ceremony and reception audio setup
  • Coordinate with other vendors (photographer, DJ, etc.)
  • Schedule technical rehearsal

1 Month Before

  • Confirm all AV arrangements
  • Finalize timeline and logistics
  • Prepare backup plans for weather (outdoor weddings)
  • Coordinate with venue for setup times

Common Wedding AV Mistakes to Avoid

1. Underestimating Audio Needs

We get it—lighting is sexy, and projectors are cool. But if your guests can't hear the vows? They'll remember that forever (and not in a good way). Audio first, always.

2. Not Testing Equipment

Never, ever skip the tech rehearsal. We test everything twice—once during setup, once during rehearsal. It's the difference between "flawless" and "oops, the mic's not working."

3. Ignoring Backup Plans

Murphy's Law loves weddings. That's why we bring backup mics, backup cables, backup everything. If something fails, you'll never know—because we've already swapped it out.

4. Poor Coordination with Other Vendors

Your AV team, photographer, DJ, and planner all need to be on the same page. We coordinate with everyone ahead of time so there are no surprises on game day.

Budget Considerations for Wedding AV

Let's talk numbers. Wedding AV isn't cheap, but it's also not where you want to cut corners. Here's what to expect:

  • Basic ceremony audio: $500-$1,500
  • Full reception sound and lighting: $2,000-$5,000
  • Premium AV with live streaming: $5,000-$10,000+

Yes, it's an investment. But so is everything else about your wedding. The difference? Good AV ensures all those other investments—the venue, the flowers, the photographer—actually shine the way they're supposed to.

The Bottom Line

Your wedding is one day. You can't redo it. You can't fix bad audio in post. You can't ask your guests to "just imagine" what the lighting was supposed to look like. It has to be right the first time.
