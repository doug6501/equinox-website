About

Event production with character.

Our Story is in the Work.

Equinox started in Manchester, Vermont — a small crew that treats event production like hospitality, not a black box. Better gear helps, but so does showing up early, listening hard, and staying calm when the plan changes.

We're still based here. Our work takes us across New England and beyond. Same crew, same standard — whether it's a museum gala, a resort wedding, or a Friday conference call that grew into something bigger than expected.

How we work

The Equinox Process

Six steps from the first email to load-out. No mystery about what happens next, what we need from you, or how long each part takes.

  1. 1 You reach out

    Tell us the type of event, when, where, and roughly how many guests. If you don't know everything yet, say so — that's useful too.

  2. 2 Discovery call

    A short call about the event you're producing and the audience you're serving. No sales pressure — sometimes we recommend you do it in-house.

  3. 3 Site walk-through

    For venues we haven't worked, we visit: power, sight-lines, cable paths. This is the step most vendors skip. We don't.

  4. 4 Custom proposal

    A written proposal sized to your event — every line of gear, crew, hours, and cost. Ways to spend less or more, named clearly.

  5. 5 Pre-production

    Run-of-show, vendor coordination, shop testing, and backup gear for anything critical. Quiet on your end — we're working.

  6. 6 Show day

    Early arrival, quiet setup, every cue rehearsed before guests. When something shifts, we solve it before you notice.
