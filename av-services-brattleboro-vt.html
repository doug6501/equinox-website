Event Production in Brattleboro

Brattleboro is unlike anywhere else in Vermont. Perched where the West River meets the Connecticut River, this town pulses with creative energy—galleries, independent bookstores, farm-to-table dining, and a performing arts scene that punches well above its weight. It's the kind of place where a Saturday might include a gallery opening downtown, a wedding reception in a converted mill, and a fundraising gala at the Latchis Theatre. Each of those events needs production that matches the community's high standards for quality and authenticity, and that's exactly what Equinox Audio Visuals delivers.

The Latchis Theatre, a historic Art Deco landmark on Main Street, is one of southeastern Vermont's most distinctive event venues. Its ornate architecture and intimate balcony seating create an atmosphere that demands AV production with finesse—sound that fills the room without overwhelming the space, lighting that enhances the historic murals and decorative details, and projection that integrates cleanly with the stage. Beyond the Latchis, Brattleboro's Connecticut River valley setting offers outdoor event spaces with sweeping views, while the town's collection of renovated industrial buildings provides raw, character-rich interiors for receptions, conferences, and celebrations.

Brattleboro also sits at a natural crossroads, drawing event attendees from Keene, New Hampshire, the Pioneer Valley of Massachusetts, and communities throughout southern Vermont. This tri-state positioning makes it a popular choice for regional conferences, retreats, and gatherings that need a central, accessible location with genuine New England character. Our team understands the logistics of producing events in this region—the venue quirks, the seasonal considerations, and the expectations of audiences who appreciate craftsmanship in every detail.

Our Services in Brattleboro

Sound & Audio
Thoughtfully designed sound for Brattleboro's eclectic venues. From the reverberant halls of the Latchis to outdoor riverside ceremonies, we deliver clear, balanced audio that respects each space's character.
Lighting Design
Custom lighting that enhances Brattleboro's unique architectural spaces. Theatrical washes for the Latchis stage, warm uplighting for mill-building receptions, and elegant outdoor lighting for riverside celebrations.
Video & Projection
Projection and display solutions for conferences, galas, and celebrations. Presentation systems for regional meetings, photo montages for weddings, and large-format video for performances and lectures.
