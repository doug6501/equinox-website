How to Rock Your Next Zoom Meeting with These AV Tips

Transform your virtual meetings with professional audio visual techniques. Learn about microphones, cameras, lighting, and software that will make your Zoom meetings engaging and professional.

Written by Doug Kunnath
Technology 6 min read

In today's remote work environment, professional video conferencing has become essential. Whether you're leading team meetings, client presentations, or virtual events, the quality of your audio visual setup can make or break your professional image.

With over 20 years of experience in professional audio visual production, we've learned that great virtual meetings require the same attention to detail as in-person events. Here's how to elevate your Zoom meetings from amateur to professional.

Essential Audio Equipment for Professional Zoom Meetings

1. Professional Microphones

Your built-in laptop microphone won't cut it for professional meetings. Here's what you need:

  • USB Microphones: Blue Yeti, Audio-Technica AT2020USB+, or Shure MV7 for excellent sound quality
  • Headset Microphones: Plantronics Voyager 5200 or Jabra Evolve 75 for hands-free operation
  • Wireless Systems: Shure BLX or Sennheiser EW 100 G4 for freedom of movement

2. Audio Interface and Processing

For the ultimate professional setup:

  • Audio Interface: Focusrite Scarlett Solo or PreSonus AudioBox for better sound processing
  • Noise Gate: Use software like Krisp or hardware processors to eliminate background noise
  • EQ and Compression: Fine-tune your voice for broadcast-quality sound

Professional Video Setup

1. Camera Selection and Positioning

Your camera setup significantly impacts your professional appearance:

  • External Webcams: Logitech C920 Pro or Razer Kiyo Pro for superior image quality
  • DSLR/Mirrorless: Canon EOS M50 or Sony A6000 with capture card for broadcast quality
  • Camera Height: Position at eye level, about 2-3 feet away
  • Framing: Head and shoulders in frame, with slight headroom above

2. Lighting Setup

Proper lighting is crucial for professional video quality:

  • Key Light: Main light source in front of you, slightly above eye level
  • Fill Light: Softer light to reduce shadows on the opposite side
  • Back Light: Light behind you to separate you from the background
  • Natural Light: Position yourself facing a window for soft, flattering light

Professional Lighting Solutions

Budget-Friendly Options

  • Ring Lights: Neewer 18-inch LED ring light with stand
  • Softbox Kits: LimoStudio 2-light softbox kit for professional lighting
  • LED Panels: Godox LEDP120C for adjustable color temperature

Professional Setup

  • Three-Point Lighting: Key, fill, and back lights for broadcast quality
  • Color Temperature: Match all lights to 5600K for consistent color
  • Diffusion: Use softboxes or diffusion material to soften harsh light

Background and Environment

1. Professional Backgrounds

Your background should enhance, not distract from your presentation:

  • Virtual Backgrounds: Use high-quality, professional images or videos
  • Physical Backgrounds: Bookshelves, plants, or branded backdrops
  • Green Screen: Elgato Green Screen or collapsible backdrop for custom backgrounds
  • Blur Effect: Use Zoom's background blur for a clean, professional look

2. Room Acoustics

Minimize echo and improve audio quality:

  • Soft Surfaces: Add rugs, curtains, or acoustic panels to reduce echo
  • Quiet Environment: Choose a room away from traffic, HVAC, or other noise sources
  • Door Sign: "Meeting in Progress" to prevent interruptions

Software and Settings Optimization

1. Zoom Settings for Professional Quality

  • Video Settings: Enable HD video, adjust for low light if needed
  • Audio Settings: Enable original sound, suppress background noise
  • Advanced Settings: Enable hardware acceleration, optimize for video
  • Recording: Record to cloud for easy sharing and backup

2. Third-Party Software Enhancements

  • OBS Studio: Professional streaming and recording software
  • XSplit: User-friendly streaming software with professional features
  • Krisp: AI-powered noise cancellation
  • ManyCam: Virtual camera software with effects and backgrounds

Internet Connection Optimization

1. Bandwidth Requirements

  • Minimum: 1.5 Mbps upload/download for HD video
  • Recommended: 3-5 Mbps for professional quality
  • Optimal: 10+ Mbps for 4K or multiple participants

2. Connection Optimization

  • Ethernet Connection: Use wired connection instead of WiFi when possible
  • Close Unnecessary Apps: Free up bandwidth for video conferencing
  • Test Speed: Use speedtest.net to verify your connection
  • Backup Plan: Have mobile hotspot ready as backup

Professional Meeting Etiquette

1. Pre-Meeting Preparation

  • Test Equipment: Check audio, video, and internet 15 minutes before
  • Update Software: Ensure Zoom and other software are current
  • Prepare Materials: Have presentations and documents ready to share
  • Dress Professionally: Wear appropriate attire for your audience

2. During the Meeting

  • Mute When Not Speaking: Prevent background noise from disrupting others
  • Look at Camera: Maintain eye contact by looking at the camera, not the screen
  • Speak Clearly: Enunciate and speak slightly slower than normal
  • Use Gestures: Hand gestures help maintain engagement

Advanced Professional Features

1. Multi-Camera Setup

For presentations or training sessions:

  • Document Camera: Show physical documents or objects clearly
  • Wide-Angle Camera: Capture more of your presentation space
  • Camera Switching: Use OBS or similar software to switch between cameras

2. Screen Sharing Optimization

  • High DPI Scaling: Adjust display settings for clear screen sharing
  • Dedicated Monitor: Use a second monitor for shared content
  • Clean Desktop: Organize files and close unnecessary applications

Common Technical Issues and Solutions

1. Audio Problems

  • Echo: Use headphones instead of speakers
  • Poor Quality: Check microphone settings and positioning
  • Background Noise: Use noise suppression software or better microphone

2. Video Issues

  • Poor Lighting: Add more light sources or adjust camera settings
  • Blurry Video: Check focus and camera positioning
  • Lag or Freezing: Close other applications and check internet speed

Budget-Friendly Professional Setup

You don't need to break the bank for professional results. Here's a budget-friendly setup under $500:

  • Microphone: Blue Yeti USB ($100)
  • Webcam: Logitech C920 ($80)
  • Lighting: Neewer Ring Light ($60)
  • Headphones: Audio-Technica ATH-M20x ($50)
  • Background: Collapsible backdrop ($30)
  • Software: Free options like OBS Studio

Conclusion

Professional Zoom meetings require the same attention to detail as in-person presentations. By investing in quality audio visual equipment and following these best practices, you can create engaging, professional virtual meetings that reflect well on you and your organization.

Remember, the goal isn't to have the most expensive equipment, but to create a consistent, professional experience that allows your content and expertise to shine through. Start with the basics and gradually upgrade your setup as your needs and budget allow.
