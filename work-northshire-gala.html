Northshire Day School Harvest Moon Gala

Driving engagement and emotional connection at Manchester's beloved school fundraiser.

The Vision

A critical fundraiser for Manchester's beloved Northshire Day School. The goal was to drive engagement for the silent auction and emotionally connect with donors through video storytelling.

The Solution

We created a visual focal point using three large monitors synchronized to display silent auction items with a live countdown timer. During the presentation, we switched seamlessly to looping slideshows and impact videos highlighting the school's work. We enhanced the room's atmosphere with 18 perimeter uplights and ensured the auctioneer was heard perfectly with professional wireless microphones.
