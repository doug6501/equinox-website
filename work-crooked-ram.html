The Crooked Ram — Manchester, VT — August 2025

The groom’s parents had spent months building a video about their son’s life. Every guest needed to see it properly.

The groom’s parents had spent months building a video about their son’s life — childhood footage, photographs, the accumulating evidence of a person becoming himself. They wanted every guest at the rehearsal dinner to see it and hear it properly. That was the part that mattered most.

We installed a 75-inch monitor with a clear sightline from every seat. High-fidelity playback, discreet speakers, levels set for the room. The video ran at dinner. The room was quiet. People watched.

After the video, the toasts. Wireless microphone to the first speaker, clean handoff to the second, then the third. Clear audio throughout. When someone is looking that hard at the room, they shouldn’t have to wonder whether anyone can hear them.
