Wedding at The Crooked Ram

An intimate celebration where storytelling took center stage.

The Vision

An intimate celebration where storytelling was center stage. The groom's parents prepared a custom video journey of the couple's life together, requiring a presentation solution that felt personal yet cinema-quality.

The Solution

We installed a sleek 75-inch monitor to deliver a crisp, bright image for the feature video, ensuring every guest had a perfect view. We supported the moment with high-fidelity playback devices and discreet speakers, plus crystal-clear microphones to ensure every toast landed with emotion.
