AV & Event Production Services in The Berkshires, MA

Premium sound, lighting, video, and live streaming for one of New England's most iconic cultural and event destinations.

World-Class Production for a World-Class Region

The Berkshires occupy a singular place in the American cultural landscape. Tanglewood fills summer evenings with the Boston Symphony Orchestra. MASS MoCA transforms a former factory complex into one of the country's most ambitious contemporary art spaces. The Williamstown Theatre Festival draws Broadway-caliber talent to a small New England college town. And woven through all of it — the weddings at converted barns in Great Barrington, the fundraising galas at Lenox estates, the corporate retreats at Stockbridge inns — is a community that expects production values to match the setting. Equinox Audio Visuals exists for exactly this kind of work.

We've produced events across the full breadth of the Berkshires, from Pittsfield's revitalized downtown venues to the rolling estate grounds of Lenox and the artist-driven spaces of North Adams. Each corner of this region has its own personality, and we adapt our approach accordingly. A black-tie gala at a Gilded Age mansion in Stockbridge calls for understated elegance — warm amber uplighting, invisible microphones, sound that fills the room without overwhelming the conversation. A product launch at MASS MoCA demands something bolder — dynamic lighting, high-resolution video walls, and a sound system that can energize a cavernous industrial space. We do both, and everything in between.

Based in Manchester, Vermont — just over the state line — we're closer to most Berkshires venues than any Boston or New York AV company. That proximity means we can do site visits without charging travel premiums, attend planning meetings in person, and be on-site early on event day without the logistical headaches of a long-distance production team. For Berkshires event planners, venue managers, and couples who want a production partner that actually knows the region, we're the team to call.

Our Services in The Berkshires

🔊 Sound & Audio

Engineered sound systems for the Berkshires' diverse venue types — from the intimate acoustics of a Stockbridge inn to full concert-grade systems for outdoor events. Wireless microphones, ceremony sound, reception DJ support, and professional mixing for live bands.

💡 Lighting Design

Sophisticated uplighting for Lenox estates and historic venues, theatrical lighting for gala stages and award ceremonies, bistro and string light installations for outdoor receptions, and custom color design that transforms Great Barrington barns and Pittsfield ballrooms alike.

🎥 Video & Projection

Large-format projection for corporate presentations and keynotes, LED video walls for product launches and trade events, tribute and highlight video playback for galas and celebrations, and IMAG camera systems for large-audience events.

📡 Live Streaming

Multi-camera live streaming for hybrid corporate events and conferences, wedding ceremony broadcasts for guests who can't travel to the Berkshires, and professional virtual event production with reliable connectivity even at rural estate venues.
