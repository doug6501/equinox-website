Professional Event Production for the Monadnock Region

Keene sits at the heart of New Hampshire's Monadnock Region — a community that punches well above its weight when it comes to arts, culture, and events worth attending. Between Keene State College bringing a steady stream of academic conferences and campus celebrations, the beautifully restored Colonial Theatre anchoring downtown's cultural life, and the surrounding countryside providing some of New England's most scenic event venues, this region needs audio visual production that can keep up. That's where we come in.

Equinox Audio Visuals has been producing events across southern Vermont and New Hampshire for years, and Keene is a natural extension of our home territory. We know the difference between engineering sound for the Colonial Theatre's intimate acoustics and setting up a full outdoor PA system for a farm wedding in the shadow of Mount Monadnock. We understand that a Keene State commencement ceremony has different production demands than a corporate retreat at a Peterborough inn. And we bring the same level of professionalism to all of them — because in a tight-knit region like this, your reputation is everything.

What sets us apart in the Keene market is our approach. We're not a massive national AV company that treats every event like a line item. We're a regional team based in Manchester, Vermont — about an hour west — and we treat every client like a neighbor. We show up early, we communicate clearly, we solve problems before they become problems, and we leave your venue exactly as we found it. For Monadnock Region event planners, venue managers, and couples planning their wedding, that kind of reliability matters.

Our Services in Keene

🔊 Sound & Audio

Precision sound systems for the Colonial Theatre and campus auditoriums, wireless microphone packages for ceremonies and speeches, full band support for reception venues, and outdoor PA systems built for the Monadnock Region's farm and estate events.

💡 Lighting Design

Warm uplighting for barn and farmhouse receptions, theatrical lighting for stage productions and galas, architectural accent lighting for historic venues, and custom color schemes that transform any Keene-area space.

🎥 Video & Projection

Presentation projection for college conferences and corporate meetings, tribute and highlight video playback for celebrations, large-screen displays for award ceremonies, and scenic content for themed events.

📡 Live Streaming

Multi-camera live streaming for campus events and commencements, hybrid meeting production for regional organizations, wedding ceremony broadcasts for remote family members, and reliable streaming even at rural venues.
